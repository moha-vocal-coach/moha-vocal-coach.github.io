About Mohamed Nasser

Mohamed Nasser is an Egyptian singer, oud player and music teacher. He holds a Bachelor of Music Education from Helwan University (2020), has taught in international schools, and has worked as an external voice trainer at the Cairo Opera House. He teaches singing, piano, oud, guitar, drums and music theory online.
