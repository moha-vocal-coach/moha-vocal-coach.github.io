Time zone

Saudi Arabia: UTC+3, no daylight saving. Saudi time is UTC+3 all year.

Planning lessons from Saudi Arabia

Time conversion example (not a list of open slots): if a lesson is at 6:00 pm Riyadh time, it is 5:00 pm Cairo time in January and 6:00 pm in July. Egypt has recently used summer time, so the gap can change during the year, and every time is confirmed before the lesson.

Week pattern: Friday and Saturday are the usual weekend days. This is a general pattern and differs by school or employer. Source
